Brand idea
Dressed for 35°

Bangkok's hot-season afternoons reach 35°C. Every Burmelin decision answers to that number: the fibre, the weight, the cut and the colour.

Use it as the test for new products and campaigns. Does this make 35° easier? If it doesn't, it isn't Burmelin.

Test the line with ten customers before it goes on bags and signage.

Four pillars

Fabric first

Long-staple linen, pre-washed so it's soft on day one. The composition is printed on every label, including the pieces that aren't linen.

Proof: 100% linen shirts; 95–100% linen trousers; composition chip on every product.

Fit for real bodies

A size run most Bangkok labels don't carry, and tailoring in store so the fit is right before you leave.

Proof: shirts 48 to 66, tees L to 4XL, free hemming at every store.

Clear value

One price on the tag, VAT already in it, and an easy way back if it isn't right.

Proof: ฿790 to ฿1,890; 7% VAT included; 7-day free exchange.

Born in Bangkok

Designed for this city's heat, rain and air-conditioning, and sold where its people actually shop.

Proof: Terminal 21 Asok and Rama 3; Palladium head office.

Who we dress

Primary

The Sukhumvit regular

Lives or works in Bangkok. Needs shirts that survive a BTS commute and an air-conditioned meeting in the same hour. Wants two or three reliable colours and a size that fits first time. Comes back each season.

Secondary

The visitor

In Bangkok for a week, underpacked and overheating. Walks into Terminal 21 between plans. Often a larger frame that local labels don't stock. Buys three shirts at once and wants the trousers hemmed today.

These two are a hypothesis. The site copy speaks to "the modern Thai gentleman", but the size run, the store locations and the English-only website point to a broader customer. Check it against three months of sales before you commit spend (see Gaps, item 4).

02Voice

How Burmelin speaks

Like a good tailor: calm, specific and on your side. Every sentence should tell the customer something true about the clothes.

Composed

not stiffLinen creases. That's how you know it's linen.

Precise

not technicalSizes 48 to 66. Hemmed while you wait.

Warm

not chummyNot sure about the size? Bring it back within 7 days.

Confident

not loudOur best seller, in sixteen colours.

Writing rules

  • Lead with the fabric, the fit or the heat.
  • Use real numbers: sizes, baht, degrees, percentages.
  • Short sentences. One idea each.
  • Retire these words: luxury, elegance, gentleman, never settles, curated, timeless.
  • Write "Burmelin" in sentences. Capitals belong to the logo.
  • Write Thai copy in Thai, with a Thai writer. Don't translate English lines word for word.

04Colour

Blue, linen and ink

Burmelin Blue is the signature, so it works hardest when there's less of it. Let Linen and Ink carry most of every surface and save the Blue for the logo, the call to action and the bag. Tap a swatch to copy its HEX.

Pantone references are the nearest standard matches. Confirm each one against a physical Pantone guide and a printed proof before ordering bags or signage.

Retire

The site uses five near-blacks (#1C1A17, #2E2A24, #16161D, #0F0F13, #1A1A2E) and two extra greys (#8A7D6E, #788075). Replace them with Ink and Stone. One black and one grey make the brand look deliberate.

In print

Print Brass as gold hot-foil rather than yellow ink, which goes muddy. Get Linen from the paper itself: an uncoated natural-white stock looks better than a printed cream. Bags and signage are specified in Pantone, not CMYK.

05Typography

Two typefaces and a logo

A serif for headlines and a grotesque for everything else. Both are free on Google Fonts, both have a matching Thai partner, and the same two go on the website, the tags and the print.

Cormorant GaramondDisplay · 500, 600, italic
Aa Rr 35°

Linen shirts for 35° afternoons.

Headlines and short editorial lines. Italic for one emphasised word at most. Never below 24px: its hairlines vanish on phones and cheap print. Switch on lining figures for numbers; its default old-style figures make "1" read as "I". Never for prices, buttons or forms.

ArchivoText · 400, 500, 600, 700
Aa ฿1,450

Breathes in the heat and softens with every wash. Cold gentle machine wash, hang to dry in shade.

Body, UI, prices, labels and tags. 400 for reading, 600 for buttons, 700 uppercase with +0.2em spacing for labels. Use tabular figures wherever prices line up.

Barlow CondensedLogo artwork only

The wordmark was drawn from it. It's not a brand typeface: keep it out of headlines and running text, and use the outlined logo files, never typed letters.

07Photography

Calm clothes in a hot city

The pictures should prove the brand idea: a man looking unbothered in real Bangkok heat. Shoot outside, in daylight, on real bodies across the whole size run.

True colour

Shoppers choose between sixteen shades of the Signature shirt from a phone screen. Shoot with a grey card and a colour checker, and grade warm-neutral. A wrong colour is a return.

Real heat, real city

BTS skywalks at noon, Talat Noi shophouses, a Chao Phraya ferry, Terminal 21 at dusk. Bangkok is the set, not a backdrop.

The whole size run

If shirts go to 66, the photographs do too. At least one model in every shoot wears 58 or larger.

Avoid

  • Stock "gentleman" clichés: whisky glasses, cufflinks, yachts. They say luxury, and Burmelin's prices say otherwise.
  • Heavy filters and teal-and-orange grading that change the fabric colour.
  • Only showing size 48–52 bodies.
  • White-seamless studio shots for lifestyle images. Save the studio for product shots.

09Gaps to close

Where the website disagrees

The brand on this page is stricter than the one on the website today. These are the places where the site contradicts it, in order of how much trust each one costs. Fix the first four before spending money on new design.

High

1. "Luxury" at ฿790 to ฿1,890

FoundThe browser title says "Men's Luxury Clothing". Prices run ฿790 to ฿1,890 in mall units, and the about page itself says quality "is not a luxury".
FixDrop the word. Compete on being the best linen shirt under ฿2,000 in Bangkok. A claim the price tag contradicts makes everything else on the page less believable.
High

2. "100% Natural Linen" as a brand fact

FoundIt's a headline stat on the about page, but polos and tees are cotton and spandex, trousers include a 95% linen blend, and jeans and underwear aren't linen.
FixSay "linen-first" brand-wide and show composition on every product (section 06). A customer who reads the sewn-in label and finds something else stops trusting the brand. Misleading product claims can also breach Thai consumer-protection rules.
High

3. "5 Bangkok stores"

FoundTwo are Burmelin stores. Three are BICASSO units "carrying Burmelin products". The about page counts all five.
FixDecide what BICASSO is. If it's a separate retailer, say "2 stores and 3 stockists". If it's yours, present it as a sister brand with its own logo rules.
High

4. Who is the customer?

FoundCopy targets "the modern Thai gentleman" with cuts for "Thai body proportions". But tees start at L, shirts run to EU 66 (a 58-inch chest), stores sit in tourist corridors (Asok, Nana), the featured review is from a visitor, and the site has no Thai.
FixPull three months of sales: size sell-through and the share of VAT-refund (tourist) purchases. If larger-framed visitors and expats are a big share, that's a sharper niche than "Thai gentleman". If Thai customers are the target, the missing Thai-language site is the bigger problem.
Medium

5. No vector logo

FoundThe header loads a PNG and recolours it with a six-step CSS filter.
FixUse the SVG files in the asset kit and delete the filter. Send printers the SVGs, never a screenshot.
Medium

6. Type and colour drift

FoundThree web fonts plus Helvetica in the printed review cards (generate_banners.py). Five near-blacks.
FixCormorant and Archivo everywhere, print included. Ink as the only black. Import brand/tokens.css on every page.
Medium

7. Unbacked sustainability claims

Found"Certified suppliers", "transparent and ethical supply chain", with no certificate named.
FixName the certification (for example European Flax or OEKO-TEX Standard 100) and link it, or cut the claim. Vague green claims attract exactly the scrutiny you don't want.
Medium

8. Generic copy

Found"Crafted Elegance", "the man who never settles", "confidence that speaks before you do". Any menswear brand could sign these.
FixApply the voice rules in section 02. Start with the home hero and the about page.
Low

9. Unfinished surfaces

FoundThe Instagram button links to instagram.com with no handle. Store cards show "Add Photo". Three collection pages say "Content Coming Soon". The timeline has no years except 2025.
FixHide anything that isn't ready. Unfinished pages cost a premium brand more trust than a missing page does.
