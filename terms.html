7-Day Free Exchange Policy

At BURMELIN, we stand behind the quality and fit of every piece we create. We offer a 7-day free exchange policy to ensure you are completely satisfied with your purchase.

Exchange Window: Exchanges must be requested within 7 days of the delivery date or date of in-store purchase. Requests made after this period cannot be accommodated.

  • Eligible Items — Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with all tags attached to be eligible for exchange.
  • Exchange Options — We offer exchanges for a different size or colour of the same item, subject to availability. If your preferred option is unavailable, we will work with you to find a suitable alternative.
  • How to Request — To initiate an exchange, please contact us via WhatsApp at +66 83 539 8811 or through any of our official social media channels with your order confirmation and reason for exchange.
  • Return Shipping — For online orders, the cost of returning items to us for exchange is borne by the customer. BURMELIN will cover the cost of shipping the exchanged item back to you.
  • In-Store Exchanges — Customers may also bring eligible items to any of our Bangkok store locations for an immediate exchange, subject to stock availability.
  • Non-Exchangeable Items — Items that show signs of wear, washing, damage, or alteration will not be accepted for exchange. Sale or promotional items may be excluded from the exchange policy, which will be clearly communicated at the time of purchase.

We do not currently offer refunds. All sales are final once confirmed, except where an exchange has been approved under the above policy.
